CEO Highlights & Lowlights July 2026
▼  Lowlights
Dominican Republic performance softened during the quarter following the postponement of the e-invoicing mandate. DGII moved the mandatory deadline to November, removing the main urgency driver for the Facturación segment and slowing acquisition across the full funnel. The Dominican Republic remains a priority for us in H2, where we will concentrate efforts to arrive well positioned ahead of the new deadline.
Hiring for key roles has been a challenge this quarter. Several candidates for important positions declined offers at the last minute, delaying the onboarding of talent the team needs. We are strengthening our offer-closing process to secure these hires in the coming quarter.
Financial Update
Alegra closed the first half ahead of plan on every key line, with revenue of $14.3M (+6.9% vs budget) and ARR reaching $30.7M, up 31% year over year, confirming that growth is holding at scale. Gross margin held steady at 81.5%, while disciplined spend kept OpEx 6.6% below budget without slowing our investment in product. The result was a decisive profitability inflection: H1 EBITDA swung roughly $1.5M to reach $1.43M, lifting margin from -11% a year ago to +10%. Q2 reinforced the trend, with an EBITDA of $718K — six times plan — and a Rule of 40 of 41%. Just as important, net cash turned positive for the first time in five quarters, closing at $4.5M. We enter the second half tracking to ~$30.2M in revenue and ~$2.9M in EBITDA, both above budget.
✦  Highlights
We defined our three strategic priorities for H2 2026, focusing the whole organization on three fronts: building our migration machine in Mexico, one that earns accountants' trust; capturing the Electronic Invoicing wave in the Dominican Republic; and delivering a flawless experience across the three ICPs in our four core versions.
June was the best month of H1 for Mexican accountants on Alegra Connect, with the completion rate for onboarding rising to 83%. This is a significant increase from the 64% average recorded between January and May.
In Mexico we are tackling the adoption barrier head-on, with leadership on the ground. Through the quarter we sustained a cadence of Alegra Talks with accountants in Mexico City, with direct executive involvement, turning those conversations into hands-on migrations of full client portfolios.
Costa Rica confirmed the payoff of the ICP model. Despite a 156% surge in New Users that diluted top-of-funnel conversion, every downstream metric strengthened: Q2 SignUps +40%, MQLs +33%, New Logos +24%, and New MRR +31% vs. Q1. The three-segment ICP framework (Soy Pyme, Soy Contador, Alegra ERP) appears to be sharpening lead quality where it matters most — at the close.
Churn trended down across most ICPs and versions. Retention improved driven by deeper analysis of churn drivers that splits it into three fronts — voluntary, usage deterioration, and failed payments.
AI Support continued scaling across Customer Experience. The volume of contacts fully resolved by AI rose 63%, raising autonomous resolution from 40% to 70%, and capacity expanded from chat into voice support, moving toward 24/7 coverage. The efficiency gains are freeing operating leverage: we are beginning to redeploy CS talent into other teams while improving response times.
Alegra Impulsa accelerated during Q2, expanding across our four core versions. In Mexico it is showing clear impact among accountants looking to onboard new SMBs: the migration-with-close-accompaniment model has activated 80% of the accountants enrolled in the program.
AI Agent — early access, scaling in July. The agent is live in Colombia with early users: ~70 Contadores and ~50 Pymes on the in-product agent, plus 292 admin users via WhatsApp. In July we're opening it to a broader cohort of 500 users to keep gathering feedback and pinpoint the highest-value use cases. Early signal is clear — reporting is the killer use case, so we're concentrating on sharpening those tools first.
AI is becoming a habit — fastest in Accounting. Two agents now run in production at scale: the CFDI accounting agent nearly doubled its July usage and held at a new baseline of ~850 weekly users, while the payroll-to-journal agent converts at 59%, generating 1,000+ proposed entries. AI is also cutting migration friction — auto-assigning accounting uses on import and restructuring Aspel's chart of accounts into Alegra's standards, lowering the cost to switch off a key competitor.
