CEO Highlights & Lowlights 3Q26
▼  Lowlights
We grew, but not yet at the pace we expect.
Accountant churn remains elevated, particularly in Mexico.
Financial Update
September closed with revenue of $2.87M, 9.1% above budget and 39% above last year, and EBITDA of $226K, $84K ahead of plan. The beat versus budget is mainly explained by stronger local currencies, while cost discipline kept EBITDA on plan at constant exchange rates. The third quarter was the strongest of the year, with revenue of $8.49M, up 41% year over year, and EBITDA of $1.07M, a 12.5% margin compared with 0.4% a year ago. Year to date, revenue reached $22.8M and EBITDA $2.49M against a budget of $317K, as OpEx dropped to 71% of revenue from 88% last year. ARR closed at $34.5M, growing 39%, with a Rule of 40 of 50%. Cash ended the quarter at $5.17M, $2.9M above budget and the highest quarter end since Q2 2025. Holding budget for the final quarter, we project FY26 revenue of $31.1M and EBITDA of $3.5M.
✦  Highlights
Churn remained stable at around 4% throughout Q3, and has been under budget since March.
The e-invoicing wave in the Dominican Republic has arrived, and we are capturing it.
We launched the Alegra Agent, the first accounting agent in LatAm that doesn't just answer questions but executes tasks.
We filled key leadership positions in a demanding hiring quarter.
Alegra Connect has become the bottleneck, a positive signal of strong accountant demand to migrate.
The new mobile app is live, with app store ratings trending up.
AI-driven team transformation continues: Lucía, our support agent, is on track to cover 80% of the support workload, with Sales next.
